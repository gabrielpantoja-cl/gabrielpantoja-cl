# Gabriel Pantoja

Ecoinformatics & Spatial Analysis.  
Modeling how market forces shape forest ecosystems and natural capital in Southern Chile.

[gabrielpantoja.cl](https://gabrielpantoja.cl) · [ORCID 0009-0000-1868-2028](https://orcid.org/0009-0000-1868-2028) · [Research](https://gabrielpantoja.cl/investigacion)

---

### Research instruments

- **[sig.gabrielpantoja.cl](https://github.com/gabrielpantoja-cl/sig.gabrielpantoja.cl)**: an open map of rural land transactions recorded by Chilean real estate registries (CBR), joined with protected areas, soils and administrative layers. [Live map](https://sig.gabrielpantoja.cl).
